## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an organic, mindful, and tactile productivity sanctuary rooted in the core discipline of David Allen's Getting Things Done (GTD). Moving decisively away from chilly tech-corporate slate and synthetic blue tones, this design system evokes the tangible warmth of artisanal stationery, high-grade linen paper, and sunlit wood studios—reminiscent of the warm craftsmanship found in Things 3, Notion, and Craft.

The emotional tone balances unhurried calm with resolute executive clarity. Capturing thoughts, clarifying ambiguity, and executing actions becomes a grounding ritual rather than a stressful cognitive chore. Generous visual breathing space, gentle textural boundaries, and a warm ceramic palette eliminate digital fatigue.

The design movement is **Tactile Minimalism & Warm Editorial**:
- Surfaces reflect the delicate fibrous quality of oat milk, warm linen, and natural unbleached paper.
- Edges are etched with whisper-thin, warm clay micro-borders rather than sterile drop shadows or synthetic gray lines.
- Interactivity carries physical warmth: amber-fired accents gently ignite action triggers, and completion actions yield organic, reassuring feedback.

## Colors

The color palette centers around unbleached linen, warm terracotta clay, and deep vegetal charcoal ink. Every hue is calibrated to emulate natural pigments on heavy paper stock:

- **Paper Canvas & Elevated Surfaces**: The foundational canvas rests on natural linen white (`#faf9f6`). Interactive task cards and floating panels step forward on crisp pure paper white (`#ffffff`), partitioned by ultra-fine warm oat micro-borders (`#e8e5df`). Secondary sidebars and contextual toolbars rest on soft oatmeal wash (`#f3efe8`).
- **Primary Terracotta & Amber Rust (`#c85a17`)**: Replaces cold blues with an earthen, sunbaked terracotta. It serves as an active visual catalyst for primary commands, focused checklist triggers, and active time-blocking highlights without causing visual strain.
- **Charcoal Ink Typography**: Text abandons harsh pitch-black in favor of deep wood charcoal (`#262320`) for headlines and active task text, soft riverbed stone (`#716b64`) for secondary metadata and hotkey hints, and pale warm grain (`#a8a299`) for placeholders.
- **Organic Context Chips**: Context tags draw directly from an earthy spectrum:
  - **@Computer**: Warm newsprint wash (`#f0eee9`) with weathered oak ink (`#544e47`).
  - **@Phone**: Sandstone tint (`#edeae3`) with deep clay umber (`#5c554c`).
  - **@Waiting**: Raw amber wash (`#f9ede2`) with cinnamon rust (`#914d1c`).
  - **@Errands**: Soft olive leaf wash (`#edf0ea`) with moss charcoal (`#44523b`).
  - **@Anytime**: Unbleached flax (`#f2eee7`) with warm taupe (`#61574b`).

## Typography

Typography fuses the crafted editorial character of **Epilogue** for display headings and section milestones with the friendly, modern legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for workflows, list rows, and interface labels.

- **Editorial Presence**: Epilogue provides a warm, crafted architectural structure to project titles and review views, balancing structure with humanity.
- **Reading Cadence**: Plus Jakarta Sans softens the task list, eliminating clinical rigidity. Its open apertures remain comfortable across continuous daily capture.
- **Multilingual & CJK Pairing**: When displaying East Asian text (e.g., 今日待办, 收集箱, 等待), standard system fallbacks (`PingFang SC`, `Hiragino Sans GB`) harmonize naturally with Plus Jakarta Sans without jarring optical weight gaps. Tracking is restrained to neutral zero across body levels to maintain typographic rhythm.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an undisturbed focus architecture designed around three adaptive columns:

1. **Navigation Rail (Outlines & Projects)**: 240px fixed width, collapsible to an icon-rail format on compact desktop viewports. It carries a subtle oatmeal surface fill (`#f3efe8`) and a 1px vertical boundary (`#e8e5df`).
2. **Main Task Canvas (Execution Flow)**: Fluid workspace with a strictly centered reading constraint of `max-width: 720px`. Restricting line length protects against horizontal visual fatigue and supports single-task focus.
3. **Clarify Inspector (Context & Decision Panel)**: 360px contextual panel sliding smoothly from the right edge when clarifying an item or inspecting subtasks.

### Responsive Rules
- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: Complete three-pane flow. Sidebar and inspector remain docked or toggled via keyboard hotkeys.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: Inspector transitions into an overlay sheet, task canvas expands to utilize available margin space.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single-column full-width view (`margin: 1rem`). Project navigation collapses into a warm-toned bottom navigation sheet, prioritizing quick capture with a floating terracotta trigger.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through **Tonal Layering** and **Fine Ceramic Micro-Borders** rather than heavy synthetic drop shadows. The depth system treats every element as an overlapping sheet of warm-pressed paper:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Warm linen paper background (`#faf9f6`), zero shadow, continuous surface.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Task Groups, Lists)**: Pure white paper panels (`#ffffff`) resting on canvas with a delicate border (`1px solid #e8e5df`) and a faint warm ambient contact shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(70, 55, 40, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Drag States)**: Active floating tasks gain an elevated warm border (`1px solid #dcd7ce`) and a soft diffused bloom: `0 4px 14px -2px rgba(85, 65, 45, 0.07)`.
- **Level 3 (Command Bar & Modal Overlays)**: Translucent warm frosted surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), bounded by `1px solid #e8e5df` and a natural earthy drop shadow: `0 16px 32px -6px rgba(50, 40, 30, 0.08), 0 6px 12px -4px rgba(50, 40, 30, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard, infusing the tactile quality of rounded card stock and hand-cut stationery:

- **Inputs, Buttons, & Table Rows**: 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius. This gives form fields and task containers a gentle, inviting hand-feel while keeping edges tight enough for dense task management.
- **Surface Cards & Context Panels**: 12px to 16px (`0.75rem` - `1rem`), providing welcoming, natural framing for grouped tasks and inbox clarify dialogues.
- **Pills & Status Indicators**: Full rounded geometry (`9999px`) reserved for context tags, project tokens, and keyboard keycap badges, instantly distinguishing metadata from actionable task titles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Terracotta fill (`#c85a17`), white text, radius 8px (`0.5rem`). Hover transitions softly to `#b34e12`. Focus indicator applies an outer ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #faf9f6, 0 0 0 4px #c85a17`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Unfilled or warm subtle tint (`#f3efe8`), border `1px solid #e8e5df`, text `#262320`. Hover triggers a shift to `#ede8df`.
- **Micro / Icon Actions**: 28x28px squircle icon buttons with gentle amber-tinted hover states (`#fbf2eb`) for task scheduling, clarification, and item deletion.

### Task Row & List Items
- Fluid horizontal task rows (`height: 42px`, `padding: 0 12px`) structured with a clean grid: checkbox, task title, contextual tag pill, due date label, and inline action triggers.
- Hover brings a gentle background wash (`#f7f5f0`) and illuminates secondary action affordances.
- Keyboard-selected row activates an interior left boundary of `3px solid #c85a17` and a soft background shift to `#fdfbf7`.

### Checkboxes (GTD Completion)
- Custom circular indicators (`18px` diameter) styled with a `1.5px` border in `#a8a299`.
- Hover preview displays a pale terracotta tint (`#fbf2eb`) inside the ring.
- Check state: Smooth spring fill into `#c85a17` with an engraved white checkmark icon, followed by an immediate gentle strike-through and transition of task text to `#a8a299`.

### Context & Status Badges
- Fully pill-shaped badges (`height: 22px`, `padding: 0 8px`, font size `11px`, font weight `500`):
  - **@Computer**: Background `#f0eee9`, text `#544e47`.
  - **@Phone**: Background `#edeae3`, text `#5c554c`.
  - **@Waiting**: Background `#f9ede2`, text `#914d1c`.
  - **@Errands**: Background `#edf0ea`, text `#44523b`.
  - **@Anytime**: Background `#f2eee7`, text `#61574b`.

### Input Fields & Inbox Capture
- **Quick-Add Canvas Field**: Borderless pure typography field with `#262320` text and `#a8a299` placeholder. Subdued key hint badges (`Tab` for metadata, `Enter` to log) render in muted warm chips.
- **Standard Input**: White background (`#ffffff`), border `1px solid #e8e5df`, radius `8px`. Active state transitions border to `#c85a17` with a subtle glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 90, 23, 0.12)`.

### Clarify Decision Cards
- Structured 2-step GTD decision surface: "Is this actionable?" with clear, weighted choices ("Yes: Next Action / Project" vs. "No: Trash / Someday / Reference"). Wrapped in pure white paper containers with warm dividers (`#e8e5df`).
